With the Kids!

The family behind Ramiro's believe parent and child communication is needed now in our era of iPads and extensive video game play more than ever before. In order to encourage families to do so, we have comprised a list of conversation starters that will be continuely updated. Thank you for choosing Ramiro's as your family table today.

Imagination and Dreams

• If you could invent anything, what would it do?

• What adventure would you like to go on?

• If you could make one rule for the world, what would it be?

• What do you think your future self would thank you for?

• If you had a superpower that could help people, what would it be?

Building Connection

• What made you laugh today?

• What was your favorite part of today?

• What do you want me to remember about today?

• What is your favorite memory of us?

• If we had a whole day together, how whould you want to spend it?

Understanding Their Perspective

• What makes you feel loved?

• What makes you feel brave?

• What makes you feel lonely?

• What makes you feel safe?

• What makes you feel excited?

School and Learning

• What is the most interesting thing you learned today?

• What was the hardest part about school today?

• Who did you spend time with today at school?

• What made school fun today?

• Is there a subject you would like help to better understand?

Character and Values

• What does it mean to be a good friend?

• When was it hard to make the right choice?

• What's something you're proud of that no one else noticed?

• Who do you look up to, and why?

• How did you help someone today?
